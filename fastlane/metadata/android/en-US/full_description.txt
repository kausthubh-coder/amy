Amy is an Android calorie tracker where logging feels like writing one food per line.

Type a food or meal, press Enter, and Amy turns it into editable calories and macros. You can scan packaged foods through Open Food Facts, estimate food photos or nutrition labels with your own OpenRouter key, save repeat meals, swipe between days, track weight, and export or import your data as JSON.

Core principles:

- Local-first diary: entries, saved meals, goals, corrections, and weight logs stay on this device.
- No account required.
- No ads or tracking SDKs.
- Manual logging works without an API key or any cloud AI.
- Barcode lookup uses Open Food Facts.
- Optional cloud AI via OpenRouter: only if you paste your own key in Settings. This is not required for daily logging. When used, estimates may include OpenRouter web search.
- Speech-to-text dictation, when you tap the mic, may use the Google speech service provided by the device. The app stays usable without dictation.
